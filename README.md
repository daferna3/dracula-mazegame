# DRACULA
A Dracula-themed 3D maze game created with Python, pygame, and OpenGL. Features a simple combat system.

### NOTE: IT IS RECOMMENDED TO PLAY IN THE DARK WITH YOUR BRIGHTNESS ALL THE WAY UP.
IT MAY BE IMPOSSIBLE TO SEE ANYTHING OTHERWISE UNTIL YOU GET THE LANTERN.


## GOAL:
- Reach the center of the maze without being beat by one of the many enemies (obstacles) in the map


## HOW TO PLAY:
- ESC to escape
- on the start screen:
	- I for instructions (as seen here)
	- SPACE to start
- navigating the maze:
	- WASD to move
	- MOUSE to look around
	- walk onto ITEMS to pick them up
		- picking up the LANTERN makes the game brighter!!
	- walk into enemies to enter a battle
	- M to open Map / see your inventory
	- N to generate a New map
	- R to Restart the current map
- in battle:
	- mash T to fight
	- if you picked up items:
		- X for holy water (instantly kills your enemy)
		- H for the mirror (halves button presses needed)

## NOTES:
- each enemy has a designated time limit and minimum amount of button presses needed to beat them
- you do not get to know these limits, so go as fast as you can
- if you can’t win, look for and uncomment a line with the words “UNCOMMENT HERE” to use a cheat that allows you to use ‘X’ for every enemy
